The Pollution Blame Ledger

Probabilistic source apportionment of Delhi PM2.5 with uncertainty-quantified health burden. Every number carries a 90% credible interval; what the data cannot separate is said out loud.

1 · The ledger

Each source's share of the episode's PM2.5, and the premature deaths it is responsible for. Deaths are episode-equivalent (see limits below) and come from counterfactual source removal.

2 · Episode timeline

Posterior-mean hourly contribution of each source, stacked, against observed population-weighted PM2.5.

3 · What is identifiable?

Posterior correlation between source contributions. Strongly negative cells mean the data only pins down the sum of that pair, not each part.

4 · Falsification

Attribution is only worth trusting if it can fail a test. The same pipeline is run on synthetic episodes whose true source contributions are known.
